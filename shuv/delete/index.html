Delete your Shuv account

You can delete your Shuv account and the data associated with it at any time. Deleting is permanent.

How to delete

Option 1: delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. In the Shuv app, open Settings (long-press the pager screen or tap SETUP) → Privacy & account → Delete account and associated data. Or go straight to the portal: https://shuv.cloud/app?tab=data.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you use in Shuv.
  3. On the DATA tab, under Delete account, type your email and tap Delete my account. You may be asked to sign in again to confirm.

Deletion happens straight away, and the page confirms when it is done. Then uninstall the app, or clear its storage, to remove the copy of your history on your phone.

Option 2: ask us by email

Email c4xbrx4lc@mozmail.com with the subject “Delete my Shuv account”, from the email address you sign in with. If you write from another address, we will ask you to confirm from the account address so nobody else can delete your account. We complete requests within 30 days of receiving them, usually within a few days, and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Shuv account, email address, plan, usage counts, credit balance and any support notes.
  • All Shuvs and replies, delivery records, API keys and connected phones (including push tokens and encryption public keys).
  • Your security log and stored IP addresses, your invitation and any waitlist entry.
  • Your Shuv sign-in record in Firebase Authentication (email, name, profile-photo link and Google account link). This doesn’t affect your Google account itself.
  • Your Stripe customer record. Any subscription is cancelled immediately, and unused credits are lost.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: your data disappears from our database backups within 30 days, as older backups are replaced.
  • Purchases: Stripe, as merchant of record, keeps records of completed purchases for as long as tax and financial laws require.
  • Abuse blocks: if your email, network or a phone was blocked for abuse, the block is kept while it lasts and removed within 90 days after it ends.
  • Deletion records: our operator log keeps a line saying an account was deleted (its random ID and the date, no email or content). If you asked by email, we keep the request (your email address and dates) for one year to show it was fulfilled.
  • Request logs without personal data are kept by our hosting provider for up to 7 days.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • API keys: revoke them in the app (KEYS) or the portal.
  • Phones: disconnect them in the portal (PHONES), or sign out in the app.
  • History on your phone: Settings → “Clear history on this phone”. This removes only the phone’s copy; it does not delete your account.
  • For anything else, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.
