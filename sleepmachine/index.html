SleepMachine privacy policy

SleepMachine (Android package com.sleepmachine.app) is provided by 13bfc3s. It is designed to work entirely offline.

Data collection and sharing

SleepMachine does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, health information, usage data, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, or audio. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or internet access.

Data stored on your device

Custom mix names, slider values, your sleep-timer choice, and your most recently played sound are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. Android cloud backup is disabled. Saved mixes can be removed in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling SleepMachine removes its local data.

Permissions

SleepMachine uses Android foreground media-playback service permissions so user-started sound can continue when the screen is off or the app is in the background. On Android 13 and later it asks once for notification permission, which is used only to show the playback notification (sound name, sleep-timer countdown, and Stop button). It never sends promotional notifications, and sound plays the same if you decline. It does not request microphone, location, contacts, camera, storage, health, or network access.

Health and age

SleepMachine provides configurable sounds for sleep and relaxation. It does not collect health information and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. SleepMachine is intended for ages 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13.

Retention and deletion

No data is retained by the developer because no data is transmitted to the developer. Local saved mixes may be deleted in the app. All local app data can be deleted through Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

Material changes will be published on this page and reflected in an app update.
